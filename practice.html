How review works

Wrong responses become due in four hours. A correct answer with a hint becomes due in one day. Independent recall on separate study days schedules reviews after 1, 3, 7, 14, and up to 30 days. Three successful days qualify an item as mastered. Three recorded misses with weak current recall qualify as a severe weakness. Earlier mistakes stay in the record.
